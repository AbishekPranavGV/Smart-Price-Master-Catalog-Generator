Smart Price Master & Catalog Generator

    Purpose: Syncs with Google Drive to pull pricing sheets, render live catalogs, and instantly dispatch quotes or mini-catalogs via WhatsApp/Gmail with QR location links and IndiaMART live market integration.

    Tech Stack: Python, Streamlit or Flask, pandas, openpyxl, Google Drive API, WhatsApp API / pywhatkit, Gmail API, qrcode.

    Key Features:

        Drive Sync: Automatically watches a designated Google Drive folder for updated Excel pricing master files.

        Dynamic Document Builder: Generates formatted PDF mini-catalogs, full catalogs, and formal quotations on the fly using templates.

        Omnichannel Sharing: One-click dispatch to WhatsApp and Gmail, embedding a dynamic QR code linked to your business/warehouse Google Maps location.

        Live Price Feed: Pulls competitive benchmark prices from IndiaMART to display alongside your internal master rates.
